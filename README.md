## Executive Summary
This report provides a comprehensive performance review of the retail organization across the three-year period (2021–2023). Utilizing Microsoft Power BI, raw transaction and master data were cleaned, modeled in a Star Schema, and analyzed through interactive dashboards to answer core business questions regarding revenue, profitability, product mix, salesforce productivity, and budget compliance.

## Section 2: Business Insights and Strategy

### Top 5 Key Findings
1. **Consistent Profit Engine:** The business converted $26M in sales into $8M gross profit at a reliable 32.52% margin.
2. **Flagship Product Concentration:** The top 10 products drive ~30% of total revenue, confirming high customer alignment around flagship offerings.
3. **Sales Team Productivity Spread:** Top salespeople sell more than 3x the volume of lower-quartile reps, demonstrating a clear coaching opportunity.
4. **High-Population Market Gaps:** Multiple densely populated California cities show disproportionately low sales penetration.
5. **Low Account Dependency:** The top 10 customers represent only ~7% of sales, eliminating insolvency risk from single-client churn.

### Top 5 Business Problems Identified
1. **Geographic Concentration Risk:** 100% dependency on California exposes revenue to local economic or regulatory shifts.
2. **2023 Revenue Softening:** Annual order volumes moderated in 2023 compared to peak 2021/2022 performance.
3. **Margin Drag from Low-Tier SKUs:** Several bottom-tier products have margins below 15%, consuming warehouse footprint for minimal profit.
4. **Sales Rep Execution Disparity:** Over 25% of sales reps fall significantly below the corporate Average Order Value (AOV) benchmark of $2,360.
5. **Location Metadata Gaps:** Incomplete geographic tags on occasional sales records limit granular local reporting.

### Top 5 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Interstate Regional Expansion:** Establish distribution partnerships in neighboring growth markets (Nevada, Arizona) to de-risk geographic focus.
2. **Sales Mentorship & Enablement:** Pair lower-performing reps with Ryan Welch and Scott Mason; focus on upselling higher-margin items.
3. **SKU Optimization:** Reprice or bundle products with margins below 20% to improve overall product portfolio returns.
4. **Targeted City Marketing Campaigns:** Allocate local promotional budgets specifically toward high-population, low-penetration cities like Fresno and Bakersfield.
5. **Quarterly Rolling Budgeting:** Adopt dynamic quarterly forecasts that adjust realistically for seasonality and historical run rates.

## Section 3: Technical Architecture & Methodology
- **ETL (Power Query):** Cleaned and appended annual sales files (2021–2023), enforced consistent typing, removed duplicates, and unified into `Fact_Sales`.
- **Data Model:** Configured a single-direction 1-to-many Star Schema connecting `Fact_Sales` to `Dim_Product`, `Dim_Location`, `Dim_Customer`, `Dim_Salesperson`, and `Dim_Date`.
- **DAX Measures:** Developed dynamic measures utilizing `CALCULATE`, `DIVIDE`, `TOTALYTD`, `SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR`, and `RANKX`.
- **Dashboards:** 7 dedicated pages supporting executive overview, time intelligence, product profiling, customer segmentation, sales team evaluation, geographic analysis, and financial budget tracking.
